Our Story

From a simple idea to the platform of the future

Kthema Gati was born from everyday frustration: every time you drop your car at the garage, you lose half the day waiting. Call after call, "it'll be a little late", "we'll finish tomorrow" — this was the reality for thousands of Albanians every day.

Our team decided to change that. With modern technology, an intuitive app and a network of certified partners, we built Kthema Gati — the platform that lets you carry on with your day while your car is being taken care of.

Today, Kthema Gati is more than just an app — it's a movement towards modernising Albanian automotive services. And this is just the beginning.

Mission & Vision

What drives us

Our Mission

To give every driver in Albania access to world-class automotive services — through technology, transparency and respect for their time. We believe car maintenance should not be a burden, but a simple and reliable service.

Our Vision

To be the leading vehicle management platform in the Balkans by 2027, connecting hundreds of thousands of drivers with the most trusted service network — and exporting Albanian innovation into European markets.
