Our Services

Everything you need
for your car

From booking a service to real-time monitoring — Kthema Gati makes your car maintenance simple and efficient.

Easy Booking

Book your car service in just a few taps, directly from the app. Choose your garage, date and time that suits you.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track your car's repair status at any moment. Get notified when your car is ready and when you can pick it up.

Certified Network

We work exclusively with certified and trusted garages. Every partner undergoes rigorous quality checks before joining our network.
