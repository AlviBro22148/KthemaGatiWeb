Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions?

Can I change my plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time from the Control Panel. The change takes effect immediately and the price difference is calculated proportionally.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contracts. Your subscription is month-to-month and can be cancelled at any time without penalty.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Download the app, create your account and start immediately. No credit card needed during the trial period. After 14 days, choose your plan or leave with no cost whatsoever.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Direct Debit and bank transfers. All payments are encrypted and secure.
